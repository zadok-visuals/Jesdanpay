JesDanPay exists to make one thing simple: moving money between Nigeria, Ghana, and Kenya and your suppliers in China, without the guesswork that usually comes with cross-border payments.

## Deposit

Start by funding your JesDanPay wallet. You can deposit in NGN, GHS, or KES from a local bank or mobile money account, or fund your wallet directly in USDT if you already hold it. Deposits are typically reflected in your wallet within minutes.

## Convert

Once your wallet is funded, convert your balance to CNY at a transparent, published rate. The rate you see before you confirm is the rate you get — there's no separate markup added after the fact.

## Send or withdraw

From there, you have two options: pay your supplier in China directly from your CNY balance, or convert back to your local currency and withdraw to your bank account or wallet whenever you need to.

That's the whole loop — deposit, convert, send or withdraw — designed to take minutes instead of days.
